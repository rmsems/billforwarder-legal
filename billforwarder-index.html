BillForwarder

BillForwarder is a personal automation built by Richard Sems. It reads billing emails from the owner's Gmail account and creates draft vendor bills in the owner's QuickBooks Online company, each requiring the owner's explicit review and approval. It does not initiate payments or move funds.

BillForwarder is a private, single-user tool. It is not a public product and is not accepting users or customers.

Disconnecting

To disconnect BillForwarder from QuickBooks, open QuickBooks Online, go to the Apps menu, find BillForwarder, and disconnect it there. You can also revoke access from your Intuit Developer dashboard.
