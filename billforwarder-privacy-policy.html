Privacy Policy — BillForwarder

Last updated: October 11, 2026

BillForwarder is a personal productivity application built and used solely by its owner to automate accounts-payable bookkeeping. It reads billing emails from the owner's Gmail account, extracts invoice details, and creates corresponding bills in the owner's QuickBooks Online company.

Data we access

  • Gmail: read-only access to emails that appear to be bills or invoices, including sender, subject, date, body text, and PDF attachments.
  • QuickBooks Online: read access to the chart of accounts, vendor list, and existing bills (used to categorize new bills); write access to create new vendor bills only after the owner's explicit review and approval.

How data is used

Data is used exclusively to prepare draft bills for the owner's review. No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. Data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the bill-entry workflow described above.

Data storage and security

OAuth credentials for Gmail and QuickBooks are stored in a secure credential vault and are never exposed in chat, logs, or files. Parsed invoice data is retained only as long as needed to complete the review-and-post workflow.

Single-user application

BillForwarder has exactly one user: its owner. There are no other end users, no analytics, no tracking, and no cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the application owner through the same channel used to operate the application.
